Ntfy.sh Setup

Receive instant push notifications on your phone or browser.

Security Warning: Topics are Public!

Ntfy.sh acts like a public radio frequency. Anyone who knows or guesses your topic name can read your notifications. The generated topic below is randomized to act like a secure password. Do not change it to a common word like "MikroWizard" or "MyRouter", or others will intercept your alerts!

  1. Download the App
    Install the free ntfy app from the App Store or Google Play Store. You can also use the web app at ntfy.sh.
  2. Subscribe to your Secure Topic
    Open the app on your phone, tap the + button, and type in this exact secret topic name:
    {{parsedChannelFields?.topic || 'Your_Topic_Here'}}
    Tap to copy the topic above.
  3. Test the Connection
    Close this guide and click the Test button on your channel card. You should immediately hear a push notification ping on your phone!

Telegram Setup

Receive alerts directly to your Telegram app.

The administrator has configured the Telegram Bot. You only need to provide your Chat ID.
  1. Start the Bot
    Open Telegram and search for {{ getGlobalConfigValue('telegram', 'bot_username') || 'the bot configured by your system administrator' }}. Send a /start message to the bot to activate it for your account. The bot cannot send you messages until you initiate a chat with it.
  2. Find your Chat ID
    To get your personal Chat ID, search for @userinfobot in Telegram and send it a message. It will reply with your numerical ID (e.g. 123456789).

Discord Webhook Setup

Push notifications directly to a Discord Server Channel.

  1. Open Server Settings
    Open Discord, navigate to {{ getGlobalConfigValue('discord', 'server_name') || 'your Server' }}, click the gear icon next to the text channel where you want the alerts to appear, and click Integrations.
  2. Create Webhook
    Click Webhooks -> New Webhook. Name the bot "MikroWizard Alerts".
  3. Copy Webhook URL
    Click Copy Webhook URL and paste it into the Webhook URL field for this channel in MikroWizard.
Your Webhook URL: {{parsedChannelFields?.webhook_url || 'Not configured'}}

Slack Setup

Push notifications directly to your specific Slack Workspace.

This requires creating an Incoming Webhook in your Slack workspace.
  1. Go to Slack App Directory
    Search for "Incoming WebHooks" in the Slack App Directory and click Add to Slack.
  2. Select Channel
    Choose the specific Slack channel where you want your device alerts to appear.
  3. Copy Webhook URL
    Copy the generated Webhook URL (it looks like https://hooks.slack.com/services/...) and paste it into the Webhook URL field for this channel in MikroWizard.
Your Webhook URL: {{parsedChannelFields?.webhook_url || 'Not configured'}}

Pushover Setup

Reliable push notifications for iOS, Android, and Desktop.

  1. Register at Pushover.net and install the mobile app.
  2. Copy your User Key from the main dashboard.
  3. Scroll down and click Create an Application/API Token. Name it "MikroWizard" and copy the generated API Token/Key.
