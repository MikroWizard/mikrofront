Instructions
How the network map works
Group

Groups fold many devices into one circle so large networks stay readable. Double-click a group (or use Open group) to see its devices; Collapse closes them again.

Area
One group per branch that leaves the core router. Best for big ISP networks: you see the core and its branches, and how many devices are down in each.
Router
Every router keeps its own CPEs/radios folded into it ("+12 CPE"). Good for mid-size networks where the backbone matters more than every customer radio.
Subnet
Devices grouped by the /24 network of their management IP. Useful when your sites are addressed per subnet.
None
Every device drawn on its own. Fine up to a few hundred devices.
Layout
Radial
The core sits in the middle and branches spread around it, like a star. Uses the screen well and shows how far each device is from the core.
Tree
The core on top and every level below it, like an org chart. Makes upstream / downstream easy to follow.

Drag a device to move it. Moves are saved for your account only; ⋮ → Reset my layout puts everything back.

Colours and symbols
{{ stateLabel[st] }} Discovered, not managed In MikroWizard, but not in your groups 3 Open events Link to a device that is down Link traffic (thicker = busier, zoom in to load)

Down: not polled successfully. Degraded: critical events. Warning: other open events or high CPU/memory. Maintenance: in a maintenance window.

Problems panel
Root cause
A device that is down because the router in front of it is down is folded under that router ("+37 down behind it"). One broken uplink shows as one line, not 37.
By area
One folded section per area, worst area first. Click the icon to jump to the area on the map.
Flat
Every problem device on its own line.

Use the status chips and the filter box to narrow the list; show on map applies the same status filter to the map.

Filters

Dim keeps everything on the map and greys out what does not match, so you keep your bearings. Hide removes it. Show device groups limits the map to devices in the groups you pick.
